## Critical Design In Context Highlight

# Personal Thoughts

When reading Critical Design In Context, I thought the text was very intriguing. Looking at this expanded discipline of design as a means to provoke debate and make users question assumptions, habits, beliefs, or similar lines of thought. One takeaway I enjoyed was the overlap of artistic elements and the core of critical design. Critical design challenges the aforementioned topics through the avenue of design. The outcome can seem artistic, and in some cases is deemed as such. But, the intent is not purely artistic, as it is to spawn debate and cause users to be reflective. Reading this made me think about neural links. Very few individuals specifically voiced their want for neural links to be a reality. But, as Elon Musk and other corporations explore this new technology, a debate grows not only about utility, but privacy, and the future a technology like that may create. The difference is that these products are already being developed, intended for sale at some point in time. To my understanding, critical design lies in the seeking of debate due to the aspect challenged, not intended to be sold. Thus, with neural links as my frame of reference, critical design lives within the design of the technology, but product itself is closer to regular design and market feedback.   

# Highlight
>_"It is only when read as design that critical designs can suggest that the everyday as we know it could be different – that things could change." Raby, 2008_

This clause made me think about the art interpretation more. Specifically, that objects can only be critiqued when understood to be connected to design, and that the outcome may look artistic but the intention and use-case is grounded.
